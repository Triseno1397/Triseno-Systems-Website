Design loop · trisenosystems.com revamp · bar: ohzi.io

Builder vs three critics

Each piece is built, rendered, then judged blind by three fresh critics. All three must pass. A fail sends the single biggest gap back to the builder. There is no round limit; the exit is winning, or you stopping the run.

Pieces

The bar (7 mechanisms)

  1. World is the page. Scene fills every frame; text covers 30% or less.
  2. Signature object is the nav. Glyph and hue swap per destination on hover.
  3. Travel, don't load. 2–3s tinted warp between sections, no hard cuts.
  4. One hue per world. UI is white only.
  5. Scroll moves the camera. One short card at a time, alternating sides.
  6. Skeletal chrome. Five fixed elements or fewer, 1px white line-work, orb cursor, wireframe loader.
  7. Every section ends in a gate. Never a dead end.
